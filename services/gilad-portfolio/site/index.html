Case studies

Three systems. Pick one and step through it.

10s of TB a month, watched by AI agents

Running production ops with AI agents · Alison AI

Problem
A fast-growing data platform with dozens of PRs merged a day, and too many alerts for people to chase.
What I built
A self-managed platform on Kubernetes with Argo Workflows and ArgoCD, plus dozens of AI agents that watch logs and alerts, investigate incidents and fix what they can on their own.
Result
Tens of TBs a month into BigQuery, a stable app despite the merge pace, and engineers focused on building instead of firefighting.

100% of manual ops automated

Pipelines that fix themselves · Triple Whale

Problem
Huge data volumes, rising cost, and too much manual operations work.
What I built
A new data system on GCP with BigQuery and ClickHouse, orchestrated with Temporal so every pipeline is stateful, revertible and self-recovering, with monitoring and alerting built in.
Result
100% of manual operational work automated, cost kept under control as volume grew.

40M monthly users' worth of data

A data ecosystem for 40M users · Roundforest

Problem
A new data team, and a company that wanted to make decisions from data.
What I built
A serverless ecosystem on GCP — BigQuery, dbt, Pub/Sub, Dataflow — with semantic layers, data validation, anomaly detection and cost guardrails, Looker on top, and a Slack bot for on-demand jobs.
Result
Fast self-serve analytics for 40M monthly users' worth of data, and an organization that moved toward data-driven decisions.
